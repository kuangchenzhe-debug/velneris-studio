Prototype terms

Last updated: October 8, 2026. This is a free experimental prototype, made available for demonstration and research rather than commercial or production use.

No guaranteed output accuracy

Demo mode generates deterministic storyboard templates, not AI-generated results. If configured by an authorized operator, Claude mode generates AI suggestions that require review before production. Velneris does not generate finished videos or guarantee consistent visual results.

Rights and acceptable content

Only upload or submit material you are permitted to use. Do not submit sensitive personal data or confidential third-party material to a public demonstration.

Changes and availability

The prototype may change or be taken offline without notice. Before a production release, the operator must adopt jurisdiction-appropriate commercial terms and provide a valid legal and support contact.
